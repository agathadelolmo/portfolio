Personal Inflation Calculator for Spain

Personal inflation calculator for Spain. Enter your annual household spending, household size and year, and it estimates the inflation you actually face compared with the official national rate, which spending categories drive it, and how it has evolved from 2006 to 2025. 911 calls in real time in Monroe County, NY

RStudio Shiny App Leaflet Geospatial
